About the program

The Kyudokan karate program, led by Instructor Christina Daji, helps students of all ages and skill levels learn and grow in karate. Whether you are a complete beginner or an experienced practitioner, our structured training focuses on technique, discipline, fitness and mental strength.

Why choose us?

  • Authentic Kyudokan training methodology
  • Structured syllabus for steady progress
  • Safe and supportive learning environment
  • Builds discipline, confidence and fitness
  • Online and offline options for your convenience

Karate syllabus

A structured, progressive syllabus designed for all levels. From white belt to black belt, one step at a time.

Level 1: Beginner

  • Basic stance
  • Basic punches
  • Basic blocks
  • Basic kicks
  • Basic movement
  • Combinations

Level 2: Intermediate

  • Advanced techniques
  • Kata
  • Kumite basics
  • Fitness and conditioning
  • Self-defence basics

Level 3: Advanced

  • Advanced kata
  • Kumite (sparring)
  • Strength and conditioning
  • Tactical training
  • Grading preparation
